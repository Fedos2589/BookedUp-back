# E8 clients — common plan (back, kit, front)

**Goal (epic E8):** an admin sees the business's clients with their visit history, notes and
no-shows, and picks a client when booking instead of typing a name and a phone. One phone is one
account, and an account can bring several people (a mother books herself for dance and her child,
who has no phone, for drawing). A client from the cabinet and, later, from online booking (E7) end
up as one client by phone.

This file holds what the repos share: the goal, the order, the decisions and the review focus that
crosses repos. **Tasks live in each repo's own plan** — an executor opens only that one.

## Decisions

Made by the user (2026-11-18, don't re-ask):

- **E8 before E7.** Online booking writes a booking on a client from the start.
- **A booking points at a client and keeps a snapshot.** `Appointment.clientId` → `Client`;
  `clientName` / `clientPhone` stay on the booking as «who booked, as it was then». Editing a client
  changes the card, not past bookings.
- **One phone, several people.** One table `Client` = one person. A person either has their own
  `phone` (an account holder) or a `guardianId` → a holder (a child, a relative without a phone) —
  exactly one of the two. One level only: a guardian is always a holder. A booking points at the
  person who comes; its `clientPhone` snapshot is their phone or their guardian's.
- **A known phone in the booking dialog** shows the account's people — the holder and their
  dependents — plus «a new person on this number». The card's name is never overwritten by what was
  typed.
- **In scope:** list with search, card with history, notes, no-show counter, picking a client in the
  booking dialog (walk-ins too), creating and editing a client.

Made while planning — the user checks them in this plan's PR:

1. **Who sees clients:** every member of the business — a master books their own clients and needs
   the search. A `MASTER` sees in a card's history **only their own bookings** (Decision 5 of E6);
   the no-show counter counts all of the client's bookings (that's its point).
2. **No-show counter is computed**, not stored: `count(*) WHERE status = 'NO_SHOW'`. Nothing to keep
   in step when a status changes.
3. **Search:** one `q` over name (case-insensitive substring) and phone digits; `phone` (exact E.164)
   returns that account — holder first, then dependents by name. Paging by `limit` / `offset` with
   `total`; the front shows «Show more», not page numbers (one column on a phone).
4. **No kit `Table` / `Pagination` in E8:** on a phone a client list is rows, not a table; the front
   builds rows from existing kit pieces. The kit gets only an empty state (K5). E8's «Kit: Table,
   pagination» in the epics doc is replaced by this.
5. **Existing bookings are backfilled** in the migration: one holder per `(business, clientPhone)`,
   named after the latest booking with that phone. Then `client_id` is `NOT NULL`.
6. **Out of E8:** merging two clients, moving a dependent to their own phone, archiving a client,
   anonymizing personal data on request (152-ФЗ / PDPA — with the legal part in E14), subscriptions
   (E15).

## Review focus across repos

1. Two parallel requests creating a client with the same phone → one client, one `409 phoneTaken`
   (the unique index, not just a check in code).
2. A dependent can't become a guardian, and a holder can't lose their phone while they have
   dependents.
3. A master never sees another master's bookings in a client card — neither from the API nor on screen.
4. A client of one business is invisible to another — list, card, `phone` lookup, `clientId` in a
   booking (composite FK).
5. Editing a client's name or phone doesn't change past bookings' `clientName` / `clientPhone`.
