# E22 New name, domain and logo — common plan (back, front, kit)

**Goal (epic E22):** the product gets a name that a web search finds only us for, a domain and a logo. Users who
search for «BookedUp» must not land on other products.

**Tasks are not split yet.** Choosing the name is the user's part; the code rename is split into small tasks after
the name is chosen.

## Decision (2026-11-26, the user's, don't re-ask)

- **We don't take the name «BookedUp».** The user googled it: many unrelated products carry it and crowd out
  anything about us. A new name, a new domain and a new logo are needed.
- **E22 goes before E14 (landing, legal) and E21 (deploy):** the domain, the links in emails and the legal texts
  need the final name. Nothing blocks E22 itself.

## What the epic includes

1. **Pick the name.** Candidates, then for each: a Google search of the bare word and of «word + booking»; the
   same on the main social networks and app stores; free `.com` and the domain of the first market; a trademark
   search in the registers of the first market and of Georgia (the seller is a Georgian IE). The user chooses.
2. **Buy the domain.** Needed by E21 (server, Caddy, `APP_URL`) and E14 (landing).
3. **Logo and icons.** A new logo; `public/favicon.svg`, `pwa/icon-full-bleed.svg` and the PWA icons in the front,
   the kit's `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, `public/favicon.svg`, `pwa/icon-full-bleed.svg`,
   `scripts/pwa-icons.mjs`. Brand colours (E13: `#5B47FB`, `#14142B`, `#A99BFF`) stay unless the user says otherwise.
4. **Rename in code.** The name today is in about 38 files of the back (2026-11-26, `git grep -il bookedup`),
   40 of the front and 7 of the kit: texts in the UI, bot and email messages (`src/notifications/messages.ts`),
   the PWA manifest, READMEs, `AGENTS.md`, `package.json` names, docker compose, `.env.example`, tests,
   and the generated API client's headers. Repository and package names change as a separate step, with the
   user's go-ahead (GitHub redirects, the kit's `github:` dependency, CI, local paths).
5. **Plans that name the product** (`docs/superpowers/plans/*`, the front's `docs/plans/*`) are history — not
   rewritten, except E21 and E11, which are still ahead and say «BookedUp» in their runbooks and decisions.

## Done when

- A search for the new name returns our landing first; the domain is bought and points to the landing.
- The old name is gone from the UI, emails, bot messages, the PWA manifest, the READMEs and the config
  (`git grep -i bookedup` outside history docs finds nothing).
- The name, domain and logo are in the epics doc.

## Open questions to the user

- Which market is first (epics doc, open question)? It decides the domain zone and the trademark register.
- Are the repositories and packages renamed too, or only the product?
- Does the logo come from a designer, or do we draw one with the kit's tools?
