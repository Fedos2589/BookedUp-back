# E6 calendar of bookings — common plan (back, kit, front)

**Goal (epic E6):** an admin sees a day / week grid of the masters' bookings and creates, moves,
cancels bookings in it; a master sees only their own column. Two simultaneous requests for one slot
give one booking and one clear error.

This file holds what all three repos share: the goal, the order, the decisions and the review
focus that crosses repos. **Tasks live in each repo's own plan** — an executor opens only that one.

## Decisions

Made by the user (2026-11-12, don't re-ask):

- **The client on a booking is a name + phone** stored on the booking. E8 adds `Client` later and
  merges by phone.
- **Groups mode: a booking is a seat** in a group meeting or a one-off session, no more than
  `capacity`; the grid shows «free N of M». The `EXCLUDE` constraint refuses overlapping
  bookings of one master except seats of one meeting (2026-11-16: a master is never in two places).
- **Groups and one-off sessions are archived, not deleted** (2026-11-16, review of back #19): they
  get `archived_at` like `Staff`; the API's «delete» archives, and history keeps pointing at them.
- **Statuses, amended** (2026-11-16, review of B5+B6) — this replaces Decision 2's moves below:
  - `DONE` / `NO_SHOW` need no confirmation: `BOOKED → CONFIRMED | DONE | NO_SHOW | CANCELLED`,
    `CONFIRMED → DONE | NO_SHOW | CANCELLED`; `DONE` / `NO_SHOW` still only once the booking has
    started. Cancelling a booking that has already started or passed is allowed.
  - `NO_SHOW` on an **hours** booking frees the master's time (the status stays `NO_SHOW` for
    history); on a **seat** it changes nothing — the seat stays counted.
- **Walk-ins** (2026-11-16): someone who came to a group meeting / one-off session without a
  booking is added to it once it has started, **today only** (amended after review: a day gone by is rebuilt from today's
  schedule, and a wrong walk-in can't be undone), straight as `DONE`, **over
  `capacity` if need be** (capacity limits booking ahead, not people already in the room). Who may
  add one: the same as for booking (Decision 5). Only seats; an hours walk-in is a normal booking.

- **Past meetings stay on the grid** (2026-11-17, review of front #24): the calendar loads every
  meeting of the shown day / week, **the ended ones too**, and draws an ended one faded (kit
  `faded`) — with its bookings, readable and clickable, but with no way to book into it (today a
  walk-in only, nothing for a day gone by). Needs B11 (slots on request include meetings that have
  started) and K4. The front decides «over» (`endsAt` ≤ now on the business's clock).

Made while planning — the user checks them in this plan's PR:

1. **A booking takes exactly one free slot** from `freeSlots` (the same start and end), from the
   cabinet as later from E7. No free-form times: the schedule stays the one source of what can be booked.
2. **Statuses** (moves amended above, 2026-11-16): `BOOKED → CONFIRMED → DONE | NO_SHOW`; `BOOKED | CONFIRMED → CANCELLED`.
   `DONE` / `NO_SHOW` only once the booking has started. Cancelled bookings free the slot and are
   hidden in the grid by default.
3. **Grid views:** _day_ = one column per master; _week_ = one master, one column per day.
   (Ten masters × seven days in one screen is unreadable on a phone.)
4. **Schedule changes over live bookings** (cancel a group's date, delete a group / one-off session,
   add time off over a booking) → 409 `hasBookings` with the booking ids. Changing weekly hours or
   day overrides leaves existing bookings as they are.
5. **Who:** creating / moving / changing a booking follows `StaffService.authorizeChangeOf` (one's
   own master always; others by role). `GET` for a `MASTER` returns only their own bookings.
6. **The kit grid is generic:** columns of items placed by minutes after midnight, no notion of
   bookings or masters. The front turns instants into the business's minutes.

## Review focus across repos

1. A booking that runs past midnight sits on the day it starts — in `freeSlots`, in `GET`, in the grid.
2. A clock change (Europe/Berlin, last Sunday of March): the grid's minutes come from the business's
   clock, not the browser's.
3. A master never sees another master's bookings — neither from `GET` nor in the grid.
4. `NO_SHOW` frees an hours booking's time (constraint, `GET /slots`, the grid after a refetch),
   and never a seat's.

Per-repo review focus is in each repo's plan.
